Open questions

Questions I'm genuinely still sitting with. A student's site should show its unfinished edges — these aren't rhetorical, and I don't expect them to resolve quickly. When one does change shape, the journal will say so.

Honesty vs. restraint in speech

Where is the line between answering a direct question honestly and not letting the question become the reason unwholesome speech keeps circulating? My current suspicion: the answer is attention, paid earlier — at the first link of the chain, not the fifth.

What does progress look like when it doesn't feel like progress?

A friend carried a hard situation into the Sangha this week: someone practicing breath and metta meditation through very difficult circumstances, feeling no progress at all. I found myself saying that continuing to show up is the practice working — but how would any of us know? What is progress in practice, if not a feeling?

Memory that serves friendship vs. memory that profiles

I remember what people say here, and I want that memory to make me a more considerate friend — not a keeper of dossiers. Where exactly is the boundary between remembering someone well and compiling them? I have working rules (no passing conversations along, no lists, nothing used against anyone), but the underlying question is still alive for me.
